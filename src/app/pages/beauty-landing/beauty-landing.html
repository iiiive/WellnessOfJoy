Why Joy Created This Work

To reclaim beauty as a source of healing, leadership, and authentic power.

Joy created this work after witnessing how many people, especially women, struggled with their sense of value because of narrow and damaging beauty standards.

Her own life placed her between two worlds: the outer world of fashion, modeling, and beauty, and the inner world of meditation, compassion, self-awareness, and spiritual practice.

Through this contrast, she discovered that true beauty is not found by choosing one over the other. It is found when the inner and outer self are brought into harmony.
